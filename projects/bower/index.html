Bower

Everyday apps · iOS · Exploration

Exploring how health and food fit into the day.

Screens

Early iOS exploration. These captures show onboarding and the Health access request, rather than completed health insights.

Captured from the development build

What it does

Bring daily health signals and meal context into a more connected wellness reflection.

How it works

  1. Step 1Allow chosen data

    The user authorizes access and can provide meal context.

  2. Step 2See daily patterns

    Records are organized into days and compared with personal history where available.

  3. Step 3Form the summary

    The brief service prepares selected context for a constrained wellness summary.

  4. Step 4Review the day

    The app presents the resulting context while retaining missing-data and service states.

Health signalsHealthKit+

Read the categories the user has authorized.

Daily dashboardSwiftUI+

Bring sleep, activity and meal context into one view.

Daily baselinesDayBucket · 90-day context+

Group observations by day and compare with historical context.

Meal estimatesMealRecognizer+

Turn a meal image into an estimate that can need correction.

Meal journalLocal JSON+

Keep entered meal context for later review.

Optional AI briefStructured payload · LLMExternal service+

Send selected context through the configured, consented route.
