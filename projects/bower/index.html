Exploring how health and food fit into the day.

Bring daily health signals and meal context into a more connected wellness reflection.

Early iOS exploration. These captures show onboarding and the Health access request, rather than completed health insights.

How it works

  1. 01

    Allow chosen data

    The user authorizes access and can provide meal context.

  2. 02

    See daily patterns

    Records are organized into days and compared with personal history where available.

  3. 03

    Form the summary

    The brief service prepares selected context for a constrained wellness summary.

  4. 04

    Review the day

    The app presents the resulting context while retaining missing-data and service states.

The product idea

Put daily health signals in context.

Look at sleep, activity and meals together, while keeping observations separate from estimates.

  1. Context

    Use the day as the unit

    Bring authorized health signals into daily groups and compare them with personal history.

  2. Uncertainty

    Leave room to correct an estimate

    Treat photo-derived nutrition as an estimate and preserve meal context for later review.

  3. Trust

    Keep missing data visible

    Distinguish a missing observation from zero, and use selected context for AI summaries only through an agreed route.

ExplorationWellness exploration with daily aggregation and meal estimates; not a clinical assessment.
