Project concept · work in progress

What it does

Make water scheduling understandable as a turn-based puzzle of supply, timing and consequence.

How it works

  1. Step 1Arrange the system

    The player changes the allowed controls before resolving a day.

  2. Step 2Run the day

    The core advances scheduled units of water through a defined tick order.

  3. Step 3Read the outcome

    The simulator records deliveries, releases, flooding and unmet needs.

  4. Step 4Adjust & retry

    The UI animates the recorded moves and exposes the resulting state for the next decision.
