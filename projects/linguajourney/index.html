Lingua Journey

Everyday apps · iOS + WeChat · Independent project

Practise what you’d say in a real conversation.

Screens

Captured from the development build

What it does

Help a learner complete a realistic language task, receive useful feedback, and bring weak expressions back into later practice.

How it works

  1. Step 1Choose a mission

    A briefing sets the situation, language and completion goals.

  2. Step 2Have a conversation

    Microphone audio is converted into transport-ready packets; the active live path uses the Qwen relay.

  3. Step 3Review the attempt

    Conversation turns and mission context become feedback and candidate learning material.

  4. Step 4Practice again

    Saved cards can become future review targets and return to the next live conversation.
