The hard parts, and what I did

Make one conversation prepare you for the next.

Practise a specific situation, then bring useful expressions into the next attempt. The idea is to make practice build on itself.

  1. A conversation at human speed

    The problem
    Scripted drills feel nothing like ordering a coffee. A live conversation needs audio going both ways with turn-taking that doesn’t stall.
    What I did
    Microphone audio is converted to PCM packets and streamed through a small WebSocket relay; the app coordinates who is speaking and plays the reply as it arrives.
    Result
    AVAudioEngine capture and playback, a Node.js relay, and a mission-based session flow.

  2. Practice that comes back

    The problem
    Feedback you read once is forgotten by the next day.
    What I did
    Each attempt turns into learning cards; a swipe deck brings them back for spaced review, and a listening puzzle lets you practise without turning on the microphone.
    Result
    Card library, review deck and listening puzzle all shipped in the 2026-09 build.
