Still

Everyday apps · iOS · Independent project

A ledger with room for reflection.

Screens

Captured from the development build

What it does

Pair a personal ledger with gentle reflection on how everyday spending feels.

How it works

  1. Step 1Record a moment

    An amount, category and optional feeling become a transaction.

  2. Step 2Keep the ledger

    Shared models and repositories provide the local record foundation.

  3. Step 3Reflect on the week

    Weekly transactions provide context for a short reflective sentence.

  4. Step 4Return with perspective

    The app pairs the ledger with a quieter review of the week.

Daily spendingManual entry+

Record the amount, category and context.

Quiet ledgerSwiftUI+

Move between the ledger and a reflective weekly view.

Shared foundationFinanceCore+

Reuse finance models and repository access.

Weekly reflectionHeuristics · context cache+

Build a reflection from the selected week and mood context.

Local recordsSwiftData+

Keep transactions and app state on the device.
