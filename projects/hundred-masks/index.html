Hundred Masks

Games · iOS · Development build

A small board. A changing set of choices.

Screens

Project concept · work in progress

What it does

Combine chess-like movement, merging and summoning in a paper-theatre campaign with readable tactical consequences.

How it works

  1. Step 1Read the board

    The player moves, merges or summons through a consistent board projection.

  2. Step 2Commit a turn

    The core checks legal actions, board state, resources and outcomes.

  3. Step 3Meet the reply

    A bounded opponent search chooses a legal response under its defined model.

  4. Step 4Undo or continue

    The theatre shows the consequence while turn history supports undo and continuation.
