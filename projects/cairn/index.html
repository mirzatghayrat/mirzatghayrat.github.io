Leave a voice note. Come back to it later.

Screens

What it does

Create a place to speak, keep a moment, and revisit it without confusing a person’s original words with a generated reply.

How it works

  1. Step 1Leave a recording

    User words and audio enter a session with explicit speaker attribution.

  2. Step 2Keep the memory

    The app stores the recorded moment and related conversation rows.

  3. Step 3Return to it

    Past-self prompts select and attribute context around the chosen moment.

  4. Step 4Begin a dialogue

    Generated words are kept distinct from the original recording and its author.
