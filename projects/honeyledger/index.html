HoneyLedger

One ledger, two people’s spending.

Give two people a shared way to discuss everyday spending, with room for ownership and personal context.

Captured from the development build

How it works

  1. 01

    Record & attribute

    Record an amount and label who the entry belongs to.

  2. 02

    Keep the details

    Local records provide a consistent basis for totals and ledger views.

  3. 03

    See it together

    Transactions are grouped into individual and joint context.

  4. 04

    Reflect on the week

    A short weekly reflection draws from the recorded shared context.

The product idea

A shared view, with room for two people.

Shared spending still has personal context. Keep ownership visible so a common ledger can support that conversation.

  1. Data meaning

    Make ownership explicit

    Attach an ownership label to each transaction, preserving who a record belongs to within the ledger.

  2. Perspective

    Let reflection follow the viewpoint

    Use the selected ownership context for reflection, giving the same ledger different personal readings.

  3. Scope

    Be precise about what is shared

    Keep local ownership labels distinct from multi-account cloud sync, so the product does not imply a connection it lacks.

Independent projectLocal ownership labels and reflections are implemented; two-account cloud sync is not established.
