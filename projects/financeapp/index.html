Finance App

From a receipt to a reviewed record.

Make everyday financial records easier to capture, organize and review, including a reviewable path from an image to suggested transactions.

Captured from the development build

How it works

  1. 01

    Capture

    The user selects or captures an image; the app prepares a bounded JPEG payload.

  2. 02

    Extract a suggestion

    The provider returns structured candidate transactions from the image.

  3. 03

    Review the fields

    The user inspects amount, date, category and merchant before committing entries.

  4. 04

    Confirm & save

    Selected candidates become local transaction records.

The product idea

Let AI suggest. Let the person confirm.

A receipt can reduce typing, but its extracted fields should remain reviewable before they become financial records.

  1. Automation

    Turn the image into a draft

    Prepare the receipt image and parse candidate transactions through a configured vision model.

  2. Human control

    Put review before saving

    Let the person inspect and change dates, amounts and categories before accepting a transaction.

  3. Data integrity

    Keep confirmed records separate

    Store accepted transactions in the app’s ledger, separating model output from the saved financial record.
