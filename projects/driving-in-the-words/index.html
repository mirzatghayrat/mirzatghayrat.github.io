Driving in the Words

A poem you can wander into.

Make a poem something a person can move through, using space, sound and an unhurried interaction.

How it works

  1. Guide a figure

    Touch input becomes a target in the scene’s world space.

  2. Move through the frame

    The figure advances toward the target and triggers walking feedback.

  3. Encounter the poem

    Chapter data supplies text, framing, atmosphere and environmental elements.

  4. Keep your progress

    Sound supports the scene while local progress records the journey state.

The product idea

Make a poem a place to move through.

Explore how space, sound and slow movement can carry a poem beyond the page.

  1. Narrative

    Give words a setting

    Organize authored chapters around scenes, characters and text, placing the poem inside an environment.

  2. Interaction

    Let movement set the pace

    Map touch input to movement in the world, giving the reader a way to progress through the setting.

  3. Continuity

    Build continuity across chapters

    Use chapter definitions and local progress to connect individual scenes into a journey that can be resumed.
