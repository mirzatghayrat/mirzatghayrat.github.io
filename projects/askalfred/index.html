AskAlfred

Continue your research on your phone.

Make Alfred’s research conversation and portfolio context accessible through a native mobile interface.

How it works

  1. Ask a question

    A user enters a question or uses the speech-input surface.

  2. Send securely

    The client calls IndexWise using its saved session token.

  3. Read the stream

    The backend provides research events and response text as a stream.

  4. Read or listen

    Ordered events update the interface; speech output is handled separately.

The product idea

Keep the research going on your phone.

Bring Alfred’s research conversation to a native mobile interface, with the connection and reply state visible.

  1. Product scope

    Reuse the research capability

    Keep research execution in the IndexWise backend and use the native app for conversation and context.

  2. Feedback

    Make streamed replies coherent

    Assemble complete messages from the event stream and show connection state alongside the reply.

  3. Mobile use

    Adapt input to the phone

    Support text and speech input with spoken replies, while keeping login tokens in the system keychain.
