From customer needs to product decisions.

I’m an Account Executive for Greater China at Remerge, working with app and game publishers on retargeting and incremental growth. My day-to-day work includes developing accounts, managing key relationships and connecting publishers’ growth goals with what the product can do.

I started in F&B supply, then moved through digital assets, data services and ad intelligence. The products and buying decisions differed in each industry. What customers need to solve, and how they decide whether an investment is worthwhile, are questions I keep coming back to.

I don’t come from an engineering background. Building my own apps and games with AI has given me a more direct role in deciding what to build, how it should feel and which features matter. Arslan and the other projects here are the results of those experiments, each shown at its current stage.
