What building Arslan changed about how I think.

做 Arslan 改变了我怎么想事情。

Building Arslan changed how I think about context, reliability and the direction I give AI.

Adapted from a reflection first published on LinkedIn ↗

I work in mobile advertising and DSP, without an engineering background. After building a few small tools with AI, I started asking whether they were actually useful.

Arslan became a way to understand agents by building one. It made some abstract questions much more concrete.

From using agents to shaping them.

The host-agent approach came from wanting to shape specialized agents around my own workflow. The interesting question became how to give a system the right context and direction.

Context should lead somewhere.

Knowledge alone is rarely the bottleneck. I began thinking about a second brain that could help with reasoning, decisions and execution.

Reliability is part of the product.

A system that behaves consistently is more useful than one that occasionally does something impressive. Constraints and predictability became central to the work.

Clarity comes from the work.

I used to assume clarity came before execution. Building, testing and adjusting changed that. AI can help with execution, but judgment and direction still matter.
