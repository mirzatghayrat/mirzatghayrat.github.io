Unmapped · Privacy

Your journey stays with you.

Unmapped is an offline, single-player game by Aralem Studio. No account is required. The game has no advertising, tracking, or developer-operated network analytics service.

For version 1.0 · Updated 6 October 2026

  • In short
  • Offline and single-player
  • No account required
  • No ads or tracking
  • Progress stays on your device

01Information on your device

The game saves your current journey, completed chapter collection, settings, and learned hand guides on your device. It may also create a local video cache for the ending. Unmapped does not automatically send these files or gameplay progress to Aralem Studio and does not provide its own account-based cloud save service.

Device backups may include local game data, depending on your settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; restoring a device backup may restore that data.

02Development and TestFlight versions

Development and TestFlight versions may create a local playtest report. It can contain chapter and checkpoint numbers, positions inside the game, session timing, interaction events, and touch coordinates. These are game and screen coordinates, not your real-world location.

The report is not uploaded automatically. A tester can choose Export playtest log in the pause menu and select a destination in the system share sheet. Normal App Store production installations do not enable this test recording.

03When you contact us

If you email support@aralem.dev, the sender name and email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include are delivered to our support mailbox. We use that information to respond to your request and investigate problems you report. Sending screenshots, recordings, or playtest reports is optional; review them before sending and leave out unrelated personal information.

Support correspondence is processed by the email services used to send, deliver, and store the message. We retain it while needed to handle the request, maintain a useful record of the reported problem, or meet applicable legal obligations. To ask about information you sent or request its deletion, contact the same support address. Information shared with a different recipient through the system share sheet is handled by the recipient and the service you choose.

04Permissions and Apple services

The game does not request access to your contacts, real-world location, camera, or microphone. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, crash, or diagnostic information through its own platform services and settings. Those services are separate from a developer-built gameplay analytics system.

05Websites you choose to open

Credit, license, support, and privacy links can open an external website. Your browser and the destination website then process the request under their own policies. These privacy and support pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, or embedded third-party media. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and request information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy statement to deliver, operate, and secure the site.

06Policy changes and questions

We will update this page if these practices change. For support or privacy questions, email support@aralem.dev.
