Getting started

Hold your iPhone in landscape and tap OPEN THE NOTEBOOK. If you already have a saved journey, tap KEEP EXPLORING.

Move
Slide and hold with your left thumb.
Small actions
Tap with your right thumb when an action is available.
Jump
Swipe up with your right thumb.
Rulers
Hold to grip, then drag along the ruler to resize it. Drag right to lower an upright ruler; drag up to raise a horizontal ruler. Release to settle, or release while waiting to cancel.
Ladders
Slide your right thumb up or down. Release to pause; keep sliding to step off.

Make the controls comfortable

The pause menu includes Show buttons, Show action hints, and First-time hand guides. Choose Show the hand guides again to replay the guides. You can also adjust Music, Ambience, Haptics, and Gentler motion there.

Common questions

I am stuck in one part. How do I retry?

Pause and choose Try this part again. Choose Back to the page to resume, or Close the notebook to return to the title.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are saved on your device. Unmapped does not have its own account-based cloud save service. Your device backup settings may affect whether progress can be restored. Deleting the app removes its local game data, so contact support before uninstalling to troubleshoot a save problem.

What does START AGAIN do?

After you confirm, it replaces your current journey. Your completed chapter collection remains available.

Is the whole game free? Does it work offline?

Yes. All ten chapters are included, with no ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or account required. The game can be played offline. Opening websites or sending an email needs an internet connection.

What can I send with a TestFlight report?

Development and TestFlight versions may offer Export playtest log in the pause menu. The report can help us understand the chapter, checkpoint, and recent actions. It stays on your device until you choose to share it. Review what you are sending and select the recipient yourself.

Which devices are supported?

Unmapped is designed for iPhone in landscape and requires iOS 17 or later.
